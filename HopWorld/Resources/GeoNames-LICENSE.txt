Chinese city names derived from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/).
Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This application filters GeoNames cities500 populated places with available
Chinese alternate names, normalizes search aliases, and converts Chinese names
to simplified characters when displaying them. GeoNames does not endorse this
application. The original data is supplied without warranties of accuracy,
timeliness or completeness. The snapshot date is recorded in the optional
ChineseCities.json database. Developer-generated snapshots also include source
SHA-256 checksums.

Download and generate the optional library in Settings, or regenerate with
Scripts/update_chinese_cities.py. Neither the raw downloads nor the optional
database is included in the application bundle.
